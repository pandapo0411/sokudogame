Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sokudo Game handles information when you use the app. Sokudo Game is developed by Hung Le.

Information we collect

Sokudo Game does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, or payment information.

The app stores gameplay information such as best completion times, scores, and settings locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this gameplay information.

Advertising

Sokudo Game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This information may be used to deliver, measure, personalize where permitted, and protect advertising.

Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage advertising preferences or reset your advertising ID through your Android device settings. Depending on your location, Google may also present privacy choices or consent controls before ads are requested.

How information is used

Local gameplay information is used only to provide game features such as statistics and best-time records. Information processed by advertising providers is used for advertising, ad delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service performance as described by those providers.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The app may share information with Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK as necessary to provide advertisements and the related services described above. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or others.

Children's privacy

Sokudo Game is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us and we will address the request where applicable.

Data retention and security

Gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Third-party advertising providers retain data according to their own policies. While we take reasonable steps to keep the app secure, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the app, advertising services, or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the "Last updated" date.
